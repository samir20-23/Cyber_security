### **CTF Cryptography**  
You’ll face challenges related to encryption, hashing, and encoding. Focus on:  

#### **1. Basic Ciphers**  
- **Caesar Cipher** (Shift letters)  
- **ROT13** (Common variation of Caesar)  
- **Vigenère Cipher** (Uses a key)  

#### **2. Modern Encryption**  
- **AES, RSA, DES** (Know how they work & common attacks)  
- **Padding Oracle Attacks** (AES-CBC weaknesses)  
- **RSA Attacks** (Common modulus, small exponent attacks)  

#### **3. Hashing & Encoding**  
- **MD5, SHA1, SHA256** (Learn cracking tools like Hashcat, John the Ripper)  
- **Base64, Hex, URL Encoding** (Common encoding methods)  
- **Steganography** (Hidden messages in images, audio)  

#### **4. Cryptanalysis Tools**  
- **CyberChef** (Online tool for cryptography operations)  
- **John the Ripper & Hashcat** (Password cracking)  
- **RSACTFTool** (Automated RSA attacks)
